Startup Venture • Medical Device

KAIRS: Knee AI Rehab Sleeve

🤖 TensorFlow 🐍 Python 📡 IMU Sensors ⚡ PCB Design
The Challenge

Post-operative knee rehabilitation is often unmonitored, leading to poor recovery outcomes and high rates of re-injury.

The Solution

I co-founded KAIRS to build a wearable sleeve integrated with IMU sensors. My primary technical contribution was engineering a TensorFlow machine learning model that interprets sensor data to track range-of-motion and exercise form in real-time.

Impact & Awards
  • $12,500 Funding: Secured non-dilutive grant funding.
  • Grand Champion: NC State VenturePack 2025 (Audience Choice Winner).
  • Global Runner-Up: 2nd Place at 2024 BMES/Medtronic Student Design Competition.
Synthetic Biology Research

UNC iGEM: PFAS Remediation

🧬 Genetic Recombineering 🧫 E. coli Culture 🧪 Wet Lab Lead
Project Overview

As Wet Lab Lead, I managed a team of 6 researchers to develop a novel probiotic aimed at environmental health.

Methodology

We engineered E. coli bacteria to knock out the TolC gene and synthesized specific proteins designed to bind and uptake PFAS (Forever Chemicals) from the human gut, reducing systemic toxicity.

Key Contributions
  • Organized bi-weekly lab workflows and experimental protocols.
  • Presented findings to large groups and managed sub-team coordination.
